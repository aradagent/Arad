# Content Profile

## Niche
Persian-language personal finance and currency/gold education for Iranians, from a currency-exchange and international-payments service (AvaPay / آواپی).

## Audience
Iranians dealing with inflation and FX volatility: savers deciding between gold, dollar and coin; families and students paying foreign tuition and transfers; everyday people trying to manage money in a weak economy.

## Target Platforms
1. Instagram

## Content Pillars
- Behavioral economics and money psychology (lipstick effect, anchoring, herd behavior, loss aversion)
- Gold vs dollar vs coin: practical comparisons and buying tips
- International transfers and foreign tuition payments (حواله ارزی، شهریه خارج)
- Financial safety: choosing a trustworthy exchange, avoiding FX scams
- Personal finance and saving under inflation

- **Why:** Follower growth first; build trust as a reliable money-education page.
- **Driving to:** Instagram follows (the user asked for non-promotional content for now).
- **Promoting:** AvaPay exchange / international payment services (kept out of growth content).

Casual, friendly Persian (محاوره‌ای), practical and calm, no hype, no direct buy/sell advice. Current posts lean on emoji and promotional phrasing; growth content should be educational, story-led, and series-based. Reels use AI-generated images with a female voice-over.
